A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man Of India 🇮🇳

About

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and the 11th President of India. He played a major role in India's missile and nuclear development programs. Known as the “Missile Man of India,” he inspired millions of students with his knowledge, simplicity, and vision for the nation.

Born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he came from a humble background but achieved great success through hard work and dedication. He believed that dreams are not what we see while sleeping, but dreams are those that do not let us sleep.

Achievements

🚀 ISRO Scientist

Worked with ISRO and contributed to India's first satellite launch vehicle.

🛰️ Missile Program

Led India's missile development projects and strengthened national defense.

🏛️ President Of India

Served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007.

📚 Inspiration To Youth

Motivated students across India through speeches and books.

Famous Quote

“Dream, Dream, Dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action.”
